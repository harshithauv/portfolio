About me

I'm a software engineer at Autoverse Mobility, where I develop Autoverse Spares — a B2B platform that helps garages find, order and manage spare parts — on both Android (Kotlin, Java) and web (Angular). I own features end to end: understanding requirements, building UI and features, integrating REST APIs, testing, shipping staging and production releases, and resolving production issues, working closely with backend, QA and product teams.

Before that, I spent time in data science, building ML models that turned messy datasets into decisions. I still lean on that background — I care about clean code, honest metrics, and shipping things that actually improve how people use a product.
